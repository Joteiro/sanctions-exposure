Data tables

Definitions. A transfer is a successful native ETH transaction that carries value, or a token transfer event. Exposure is one row per transfer and sanctioned party. The listing date is when OpenSanctions first saw the entry, which stands in for the designation date. Hops ignore direction and time: they measure who transacted with whom, not where funds went.

Privacy. No address appears on this page. Sanctioned addresses are numbered by listing date, and counterparties are not identified.
